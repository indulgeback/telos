Faithful simplified Telos sidebar, chat composer, clarification, plan approval, tool traces and artifact panel. Chinese is the main language. ## Motion
Smooth 0.5–0.8 second scene crossfades, a few camera reframes, deliberate cursor movement, visible progressive text and task states. No arbitrary bouncing or spinning. 40 seconds, 24fps.

## What NOT to Do
No false performance metrics, fake live execution, tiny illegible UI, third-party logos, copied product footage, or artificial audio claims. No photorealistic AI person. Character T keeps approved cream-hood B identity.
